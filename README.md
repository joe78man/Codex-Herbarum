The Codex Herbarum is your personal codex book, here you add the plants you discover and you can check the book when identifing, gathering and using the specimen for its uses.
Click on "EN" or "ES" to toggle between english and spanish

Click on "Discover" to select the plant to unlock.
	Here you will find the entire list. By selecting the ones you discovered you add them to your codex. Arrange with your DM wich plants you discover and add them

Click on Codex to open your book
	Here you will see the discovered ones, this are those you actually know how they look, where to find them and how to use their properties

Starting point:
	As a herborist you trained with common specimens and learned the basics with them.
at Level 1 or when acquiring the book for the fist time you will chose from the list as per the following criteria:

WIS+INT+prof sets the amount of Very common	and common specimens, as well as 1 rare plant. The regions should be consistent with your place of origin and background.
Add them to your Codex Herbarum.
	
Scavenge Mechanics:
Roll D100 for chance to find them in the current region
D100	Rarity
01 - 53	Very Common
54 - 79	Common
80 - 91	Uncommon
92 - 96	Rare
97 - 99	Very rare
100		Legendary

For the unknown plants (2) the DM must set the conditions for them to be discovered
